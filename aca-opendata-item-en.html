Dataset

Information on the execution of the Agency's off-budget fund estimate

Updated 03.04.2026 Category: Budget and spending 3 994 downloads

Quarterly figures on the receipt and spending of the Agency's off-budget fund: expenditure items, approved and actual amounts, variances. The data is published after the quarterly report is approved and is reconciled with the Agency's accounting records.
