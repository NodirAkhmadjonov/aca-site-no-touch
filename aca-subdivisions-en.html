Departments

The departments of the central office and what each is responsible for.

Published 18.09.2025Updated 11.03.2026

Central office departments

  • Agency Director
    • First Deputy Director

      • Corruption Prevention Department

        Developing preventive measures and anti-corruption review of draft regulations.

      • Compliance Control Department

        Introducing and supporting internal anti-corruption control systems in organisations.

    • Deputy Director

      • Monitoring and Analysis Department

        Reviewing the state of corruption, preparing analytical materials and rankings.

      • International Cooperation Department

        Engagement with UNODC, UNDP, the EU and relevant bodies of other states.

    • Reporting directly to the Director

      • Reports Handling Department

        Receiving and reviewing reports from citizens and legal entities.

      • Information service

        Official publications, engagement with the media, and running web resources.
