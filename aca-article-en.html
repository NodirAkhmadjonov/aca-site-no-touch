Work to ensure the targeted use of funds allocated under investment projects and to ensure openness has been reviewed in Bukhara and Navoi Regions

5 August 2026 Inspections and monitoring 74 views

The Anti-Corruption Agency of the Republic of Uzbekistan has reviewed work on the targeted use of funds allocated under investment projects and on ensuring openness in Bukhara and Navoi Regions.

Subject of review

The review analysed the procedures for allocating funds, the completeness of publishing information about concluded contracts, and compliance with legislation on the openness of government bodies.

Openness in budget spending is a key condition for preventing corruption during the delivery of investment projects.

Identified shortcomings

  • Failure to publish information about concluded contracts in a timely manner.
  • The lack of a unified procedure for recording changes to project documentation.
  • Insufficient internal control when accepting completed work.

Measures taken

Based on the review, the responsible organisations were sent recommendations to remedy the identified shortcomings. Their implementation will be monitored by the Agency through the compliance control system.
