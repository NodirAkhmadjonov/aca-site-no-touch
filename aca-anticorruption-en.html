Countering corruption within the Agency

The internal documents the Agency follows to prevent corruption among its own staff: policy, ethics rules, regulations and methodologies.

Published 06.01.2023Updated 15.09.2026

The Agency holds itself to the same requirements it sets for other government bodies. The documents below apply to all staff of the central office and the regional offices.
