Book an appointment with the leadership

Choose who you would like to see, a date and a time from the reception schedule. Reception staff confirm the booking by phone or SMS within one working day.

Published 15.09.2026Updated 15.09.2026

How it works

Request → confirmation by reception → SMS with the appointment time. Prototype: the request is kept in this browser and appears on the reception desk.

Appointment request

Fields marked with * are required.

Check the form

    Choose who you would like to see.

    Date *

    Choose an official first — the next reception days will appear.

    Choose a date.

    Time *

    An appointment lasts up to 30 minutes. Times already taken cannot be chosen.

    Choose a time.

    Choose a subject.

    Up to 500 characters. You can give the details at the appointment.

    500 of 500 characters left

    Describe your question — the field is empty.

    Enter your full name.

    Format +998 90 123 45 67. The confirmation SMS goes to this number.

    Enter the full phone number: +998 and nine digits.

    Optional: we will also email the confirmation.

    Check the email address — for example, name@mail.uz.

    You cannot book without agreeing.

    Request sent

    Reception staff will confirm the time by phone or SMS within one working day. Bring your passport or ID card to the appointment.
