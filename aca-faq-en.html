Frequently asked questions

Answers on reporting, income declarations, and how the Agency works.

Published 15.12.2025Updated 10.04.2026

Where to report corruption?

Via the unified platform — with identity verified via ONE ID. You will receive a report number to check the status. The 1253 call centre also operates.

Can a report be submitted anonymously?

No. By law, a report is submitted with identity verification via ONE ID. Only the Agency sees the applicant's data — it is not passed to the organisation you are reporting.

How long does it take to review a report?

The deadline is set by law and shown in the report card when status check. If the deadline is extended, this is also shown in the card.

What to do in a conflict of interest?

A civil servant must notify their immediate supervisor in writing and refrain from taking part in the decision until the situation is resolved.

Who must file an income declaration?

Officials holding positions from the approved list, and candidates for such positions. More — in the section Income declarations.

How to obtain the Agency's open data?

Datasets are available for free download in the section Open data in CSV, XLSX, JSON and XML formats.
